Search engine optimization (SEO)

SEO gets your website in front of people who are already searching for what you sell. We fix what stops search engines from understanding your site, then build the pages and authority you need to rank for searches that lead to sales, not just traffic.

What's included

  • Technical SEO audit and fixes
  • Keyword research by buying intent
  • On-page optimization of titles, headings and copy
  • Site speed and Core Web Vitals improvements
  • Content plan tied to target keywords
  • Link building from relevant, real websites
  • Schema markup for rich results
  • Monthly ranking and traffic report

A good fit for: Businesses with a website that gets little search traffic, or traffic that does not turn into leads.

Local SEO

When someone searches “near me,” Google shows three businesses on the map before any website. Local SEO is how you earn one of those spots. We optimize your Google Business Profile, clean up your listings across directories and help you get more reviews from real customers.

What's included

  • Google Business Profile setup and optimization
  • Name, address and phone consistency across directories
  • Location pages for each city you serve
  • Review request process and response templates
  • Local link and citation building
  • Map pack ranking tracking by area

A good fit for: Service-area businesses and storefronts: contractors, clinics, law firms, restaurants and retailers.

Pay-per-click advertising (PPC)

Paid search puts you at the top of results the day your campaign launches. We build campaigns around the searches most likely to turn into customers, write the ads, set up conversion tracking and manage spend every week so your budget goes to what works.

What's included

  • Google Ads and Microsoft Ads setup
  • Search, Performance Max and remarketing campaigns
  • Keyword and negative keyword management
  • Ad copy writing and A/B testing
  • Landing page recommendations
  • Call and form conversion tracking
  • Weekly bid and budget optimization
  • Cost per lead reporting

A good fit for: Businesses that need leads quickly, are launching a new offer, or want to test demand before investing in SEO.

Social media marketing

We pick the platforms where your customers actually spend time, then plan, create and publish content for them. For faster reach, we run paid social campaigns with clear targeting and track which posts and ads lead to inquiries or sales.

What's included

  • Platform strategy and content calendar
  • Post design, captions and short-form video
  • Facebook and Instagram ads
  • LinkedIn ads for B2B audiences
  • TikTok ads and creative testing
  • Audience and retargeting setup
  • Community management
  • Monthly performance report

A good fit for: Consumer brands, local businesses with a visual product, and B2B companies selling to defined job titles.

Content marketing

Good content answers the questions your customers ask before they buy. We research those questions, write clear articles and landing pages around them, and connect each piece to a service page so readers have a next step.

What's included

  • Topic research from real search data
  • Blog posts and long-form guides
  • Service and landing page copy
  • Case study writing
  • Updates to existing content that has lost rankings
  • Internal linking plan

A good fit for: Businesses that sell something people research before buying, such as professional services, software or high-ticket products.

Website design and development

Your website is where every other channel sends people, so it needs to load fast, work on phones and make it obvious how to contact you. We design and build sites on platforms your team can update, with technical SEO, analytics and accessibility handled before launch.

What's included

  • Custom design for your brand
  • Mobile-first, responsive build
  • WordPress, Webflow or Shopify development
  • Technical SEO and redirects at launch
  • Accessibility checks (WCAG 2.1 AA)
  • Contact forms, booking and call tracking
  • Speed optimization
  • Training so your team can make edits

A good fit for: Businesses with an outdated, slow or hard-to-update website, or one that gets visits but few inquiries.

Email marketing

Email is the channel you own outright. We set up automated sequences that follow up with new leads, newsletters that bring past customers back, and segmentation so each person gets messages that match where they are.

What's included

  • Platform setup (Klaviyo, Mailchimp or HubSpot)
  • Welcome and lead nurture sequences
  • Abandoned cart and post-purchase flows
  • Monthly newsletters
  • List segmentation and cleanup
  • Deliverability setup (SPF, DKIM, DMARC)

A good fit for: E-commerce stores and businesses with a list of leads or past customers that is not being used.

Analytics and conversion optimization

You cannot improve what you do not measure. We set up tracking so you know which channels bring in calls, forms and sales, then test changes to your pages to raise the share of visitors who become leads.

What's included

  • Google Analytics 4 and Tag Manager setup
  • Conversion and call tracking
  • Looker Studio dashboards
  • Heatmap and session recording review
  • A/B testing of pages and forms
  • Monthly insights with recommended changes

A good fit for: Businesses spending on marketing without clear data on what is working.
