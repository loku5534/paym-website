About Paym Technology

We are a digital marketing agency in the United States that helps small and mid-sized businesses grow through search, ads, social media and better websites.

Why we started

Too many business owners pay for marketing every month without knowing what it brings in. Reports full of impressions and clicks do not answer the question that matters: did this bring in customers?

Paym Technology was built to answer that question. We combine technical skill in SEO, paid media and web development with simple, honest reporting. Before we start, we agree on the numbers that count for your business, then we report on those numbers every month.

We keep our client list small enough that the people who plan your strategy are the same people who do the work.

How we operate

Four commitments every client gets, regardless of budget.

We measure what pays

Calls, form leads, booked appointments and sales come first. Traffic and rankings matter only when they lead to those.

Reports in plain English

Each monthly report covers what we did, what changed, what it cost and what we will do next. No jargon to decode.

You own everything

Your website, ad accounts, analytics and content belong to your business. We work inside them with your permission.

One point of contact

You get a named account lead who knows your business, answers your emails and runs your monthly review.

Your first 30 days

What happens after you sign, week by week.

  1. Kickoff

    Week 1. A call to learn your business, customers and goals. We get access to your accounts and set up tracking.

  2. Audit

    Week 2. We review your website, search presence, ads and competitors, and document what we find.

  3. Plan

    Week 3. You approve a written plan with priorities, timelines and the numbers we will report on.

  4. First launches

    Week 4. The highest-impact fixes and campaigns go live, and your first report date is set.

Where we work

We are a US company serving clients nationwide, from single-location businesses to brands selling in all 50 states. Meetings run over video, and we keep our hours on Eastern Time with coverage for Pacific clients.

If you serve a local market, we build your local search presence city by city. If you sell nationally, we plan campaigns and content around national demand.
